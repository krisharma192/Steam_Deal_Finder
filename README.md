
# Steam Deal Finder

My app helps gamers on a budget decide which Steam games to buy by comparing prices, player ratings, and the amount of gameplay they can get for their money.

> A course project for **MIST 4600: Computer Programming in Business** at the University of Georgia's Terry College of Business (Fall 2026).

## Problem
Buying video games can be expensive, especially for gamers on a budget. With thousands of games available on Steam, it can be difficult to find affordable games that are highly rated and provide enough gameplay to be worth the money. My app helps gamers compare their options and find games that offer the best value.

## What it does
My app answers three questions:
1. **Budget:** Which highly rated games fit within the user's budget?
2. **Gameplay Value:** Which games give players the most hours of gameplay for their money?
3. **Positive Reviews:** Which affordable games have the highest number of positive reviews?

## Data
The [Steam Games Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/artermiloff/steam-games-dataset) on Kaggle, collected in March 2025. The dataset is shared under the MIT License by its creator, artermiloff. This repo uses `steam_games.csv`, a smaller file made for the course: the 1,000 games with the most reviews.
